Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

About This Application

Boat AI V5 Backup is a private utility designed to back up and restore system data using Google Drive and rclone.

Google Drive Access

With the owner's authorization, this utility may access Google Drive to upload, retrieve, and manage backup files.

Data Usage

Google Drive data is used only for authorized backup and recovery operations. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Data Storage and Security

Backup files are intended to be encrypted before cloud storage. OAuth credentials are kept private and are not published on this website.

Data Sharing

Backup contents are not shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

The owner controls backup retention and deletion within Google Drive.

Access Revocation

The owner may revoke access at any time through Google Account settings.
